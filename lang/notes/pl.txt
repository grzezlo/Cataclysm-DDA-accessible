Notes for translating Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead into Polish
=============================================================

These notes guide the AI translations in lang/machine/pl.po and the
reviewers who check them.  lang/po/pl.po, the upstream human translation, is
the authority for terminology: when these notes and an established term in
lang/po/pl.po disagree, follow lang/po/pl.po, except where a rule below
corrects a known upstream mistake.

Finding existing translations
-----------------------------

Search the translation memory before naming any item, monster, faction,
skill, mutation, or game term:

    uv run --no-project --with polib python lang/access_catalog.py lookup -i "<English regex>" lang/po/pl.po lang/machine/work/pl/*.po

Add --translation to search Polish text instead.  Search for the item a
variant or conditional name refers to (the #. comment names it), and reuse
its Polish name.  Translations in lang/machine/work/pl come from earlier
batches; keep them consistent unless they are wrong.

When a name is not translated anywhere, read the game data named in the #:
reference to learn what the thing is before translating it.  Example:
"groundnut" in this game is Apios americana ("Indian potato"), not a peanut.

Address and voice
-----------------

- Address the player in the second person singular: "Czujesz, że...".
- Use the masculine form for the player when the gender is unknown.  The
  msgctxt values u:f, u:m, npc:f, npc:m, and npc:n select the gender of the
  player (u) or the speaking NPC; follow them.
- Do not assume the gender of <npcname>, <npc_name>, or a monster.  Prefer
  wording that needs no gender: "ale cel robi unik", "ale <npcname> robi
  unik", "oblewając cel kwasem", "jaką korzyść z tego odniesie".
- Dialogue should sound like the speaker, notes and signs like their writer.
  Swearing stays swearing.  Intentional mistakes in children's notes stay
  (keep about as many mistakes as the original).
- Interface text is short and uses the established patterns, for example
  debug prompts "Ustaw X na? Obecnie: %d".

Typography
----------

- Quotation marks: „tekst”.  Ellipsis: … where the original has one.
- One space between sentences, even where the English has two.
- Keep leading and trailing spaces, \n, and interface punctuation such as a
  trailing colon.  Do not add a final period where the original has none.
- Vehicle names, item names, and monster names start with a lowercase letter
  ("luksusowy sedan elektryczny") unless they begin with a proper name.
- Calibers keep their notation; casings use "łuska 9 x 19 mm".

Plural forms
------------

msgstr[0] is used for 1, msgstr[1] for 2-4, 22-24, ..., msgstr[2] for 0,
5-21, 25-31, ..., and msgstr[3] for fractions.  Item names: "latarka",
"latarki", "latarek", "latarki".

- Uncountable or collective names still need a natural form for every count:
  "garść nasion rzepaku", "garście nasion rzepaku", "garści nasion rzepaku",
  "garści nasion rzepaku".
- Book variants: "Podręcznik wytwórcy octu", "książki Podręcznik wytwórcy
  octu", "książek Podręcznik wytwórcy octu", "książki Podręcznik wytwórcy
  octu".
- A count with "co najmniej", "wymaga", or "potrzebuje" takes the genitive:
  "Wymaga co najmniej %d ładunku" / "ładunków" / "ładunków" / "ładunku".
  lang/po/pl.po has this wrong in "Needs at least %d charge"; do not copy it.

Format arguments and tags
-------------------------

Follow lang/notes/README_all_translators.md.  The validator enforces
arguments and tag balance; these rules cover grammar it cannot check.

- A substitution tag inserts text in a fixed grammatical form, almost always
  the nominative.  Build the sentence so that form fits:
  - <color> inserts a masculine adjective ("turkusowy"): write "w kolorze:
    <color>", never "w <color> todze".
  - <character_gender_adjective> inserts a noun ("kobieta", "mężczyzna"):
    write "<character_gender_adjective> z nerwową miną", never "zdenerwowany
    <character_gender_adjective>".
  - <the_cataclysm> inserts a noun such as "Kataklizm" or "koniec świata".
    Where a case other than the nominative is needed, rephrase ("odwrócenie
    tego, czym jest <the_cataclysm>").  Never add a tag the original lacks
    and never replace literal text such as "The Cataclysm" with a tag.
  - A colon before the tag is an accepted way to avoid inflection:
    "Widać na nim: <grad_base>".
- Find out what a %s or %1$s holds before inflecting around it.  Read the
  source named in #: when the comment does not say.
  - Body parts in combat messages arrive in the accusative ("lewą rękę"):
    "%1$s trafia <npcname> nożem w %2$s!", "drapie cię w %2$s".  They cannot
    be the subject of a sentence.
  - Item, furniture, and liquid names arrive in the nominative and have
    unknown gender.  Never put them where Polish needs another case or
    agreement: after a verb that takes an object ("Wyjmujesz %s z kadzi"
    gives "Wyjmujesz latarka"), after a preposition ("dla %s", "z %s",
    "bez %s"), after "więcej", "nadmiar", or a quantity ("5 litrów %s"),
    or with a gendered word ("Twój %s", "%s jest pusty").  End the phrase
    with a colon or use a neutral noun: "Wyjmujesz z kadzi: %s.",
    "Nie znaleziono przepisu mielenia dla: %s.", "Nie możesz trzymać
    przedmiotu %s.".  This was the most frequent review correction.
  - In conditional names such as "%s topaz necklace" the %s may be a
    nickname ("Big Iron"), so keep the noun: "naszyjnik %s z topazem".
- The possessive "<npcname>'s %s" is written "%s <npcname>" as in
  lang/po/pl.po.
- <npcname> and %s names stay uninflected, so never use them where Polish
  needs the dative or genitive ("utrudnia <npcname> uciskanie").  Use the
  possessive pattern or a colon: "Złamana kończyna <npcname> utrudnia…",
  "%1$s: nie udało się wykonać czynności %2$s…".
- A number that is not part of a plural entry cannot agree with its noun
  ("%d pól" is wrong for 1-4).  Put it after a label instead: "Liczba pól
  blokujących zmianę: %d", "Kursy w jedną str.: %15d".
- Hotkey labels such as "<R>emove custom color" put the letter first, as in
  lang/po/pl.po: "<r> Usuń własny kolor".
- msgctxt "gun_type_type" entries are internal identifiers; keep them as in
  the original ("glock_19").

Established terms
-----------------

Verify anything not listed here with lookup.

- merch: kwity ("[200 kwitów, 3 dni]")
- Exodii: Exodii, undeclined ("karabin Exodii", "robotnik Exodii")
- Thorazine: torazyna
- zombie: zombie, undeclined
- survivor: ocalony
- triffid: tryfid; fungaloid: grzyboloid; mi-go: mi-go
- shocker zombie: zombie porażacz; feral human: dziki człowiek
- bionic: bionika; proficiency: biegłość; mana: mana; stamina: stamina
- skills: wytwarzanie (fabrication), surwiwal (survival), mechanika,
  elektronika, krawiectwo, strzelectwo, walka w zwarciu (melee), walka wręcz
  (unarmed combat), broń obuchowa, broń sieczna, broń kłująca, łucznictwo,
  rzucanie, prowadzenie, pływanie, komputery
- wield: trzymać, weź do ręki (not dzierżyć or dobyć)
- encumbrance: skrępowanie; crafter: wytwórca; tileset: nakładka graficzna
- disassemble: demontować, zdemontowanie (not rozbierać, which means undress)
- activity levels: Brisk is Energiczna
- clothing layers: przy skórze, normalne, zewnętrzne, przyczepione
- MOLLE or ammo pouches: ładownice; leather pouch: skórzana sakwa
- matchhead powder: proch z zapałek
- bronze X: X z brązu ("pika z brązu"), never "brązowy" (brown)
- gas turbine engine: gazowy silnik turbinowy
- chest rig: kamizelka taktyczna
- Dark Days Radio: Radio Mrocznych Dni
- Mod names (Magiclysm, Aftershock, Xedra Evolved) stay in English.
